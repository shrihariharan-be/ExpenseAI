How to Use ExpenseAI

Your complete guide to managing income, expenses, monthly category budgets, and AI-driven financial intelligence.

Add Transaction Now
01
Add Transactions

Record your daily cash inflows and outflows. Choose Income or Expense, select the dynamic category, enter the amount, and save.

How it works: The category dropdown dynamically alternates based on whether you choose Income (Salary, Freelance, etc.) or Expense (Food, Bills, etc.).
Go to Add Transaction →
02
View Dashboard

The central hub of your financial portfolio. Displays total income, total expenses, net balance, and interactive Chart.js graphs.

Features: Expense by Category donut chart, Monthly Income vs. Expense bar chart, and the 5-factor Financial Health Score Index.
Open Dashboard →
03
Manage Transactions

Search by keyword, filter by transaction type, category, or date range, and edit or delete records with confirmation safeguards.

Filters: Instantly filter any combination of start date, end date, type, and keyword. Summaries update automatically in real-time.
View Transactions →
04
Set Monthly Budgets

Create monthly category budgets to control spending and receive automatic overspending alerts as you approach or exceed limits.

Alert Thresholds: Progress bars turn amber when spending reaches 80% and red with warning alerts if exceeded.
Manage Budgets →
05
Financial Analytics

Powered by Pandas and NumPy. Computes category percentage shares, monthly cashflow histories, and cumulative running balances.

Vectorized Math: Uses np.cumsum for running balances and Pandas grouping for monthly net savings.
Explore Analytics →
06
AI Insights & Forecasting

Scikit-Learn Machine Learning forecasts next month's expenses, detects statistical anomalies (Z-Score & IQR), and calculates your Health Score.

ML Regression: Evaluates model \( R^2 \) confidence and plots a historical vs. predicted trend trajectory.
View AI Insights →
07
Import & Export

Import batches of transaction records from bank statements via CSV or generate executive PDF statements and CSV exports.

PDF Statements: Uses ReportLab to generate clean executive summaries suitable for audits and personal records.
Import CSV → | Download PDF →
